## 4\. Post Indie Hackers (forum)

**Titre :** "I built an accountability app in 50h by betting money on myself — now shipping it"

**Corps :**

Hey IH community,

Quick backstory: I'd been procrastinating on a project for weeks. Decided to force myself by putting €100 on the line with a hard deadline. Shipped in 14 days.

So I built the tool that made me do it.

**Proof of Progress** — you commit to a goal, stake real money (€25–100), submit proof before your deadline, or forfeit your stake.

The mechanism is simple: loss aversion does what no to-do app can. You don't want to lose that money. So you ship.

What's built:

* 4-step commitment creation flow

* Stripe payment (real money at stake)

* Countdown timer on dashboard

* Evidence submission

* Admin review queue (me, for now)

What's not built yet:

* Automated verdict (currently manual)

* Multiple simultaneous commitments

* Referee system

Would love honest feedback from builders who've struggled with shipping. Is this something you'd use? What would stop you from trying it?

→ [https://proof-of-progress.vercel.app](https://proof-of-progress.vercel.app)
